Palming relaxation

Rub your hands gently, close your eyes, and rest your palms lightly over them without pressing.

General comfort routine only—not a treatment for myopia or an eye condition. An optometrist or ophthalmologist can tailor advice.

Safety first · ਸੁਰੱਖਿਆ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ

Use a sturdy chair or counter for support. Stop if there is sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain, or a sudden increase in swelling. For eye exercises, do not press or rub your eyes. Stop for pain, flashing lights, new floaters, double vision, or sudden vision changes. Ask a doctor or physiotherapist/eye-care professional if this routine is suitable for you.
